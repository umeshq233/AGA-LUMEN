Executive Summary

AGA Lumen & PowerGrid Solutions is a vertically integrated engineering company delivering sustainable lighting systems and power‑grid solutions across industrial, commercial, municipal and utility sectors. We combine energy‑efficient LED luminaires, smart lighting controls and end‑to‑end grid services (design, automation, integration, and O&M) to reduce operating costs, improve reliability and accelerate decarbonization.

Vision & Mission

Mission: Deliver cost‑effective, reliable engineering solutions that lower carbon footprint, increase uptime and provide measurable returns on energy and infrastructure investments.
